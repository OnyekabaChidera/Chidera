Everyone is guessing which affiliate niche to pick. Shopify just stopped the guessing.

Shopify's latest 2026 affiliate marketing analysis highlights several strong categories, from personal finance and travel to AI, pets, beauty, and ecommerce. That gives beginners a useful starting point instead of choosing randomly. Even if you have zero budget, you can start researching these markets, creating free content, and testing affiliate programs without buying inventory.

### Personal Finance

Personal finance remains one of the strongest affiliate marketing categories because people constantly need help managing, saving, and planning their money. Shopify includes budgeting, investing, credit, taxes, and financial software among the opportunities in this category.

You could create content around budgeting for beginners, saving money, financial planning, or comparing money-management tools. The reason this works is simple - money problems create urgent searches. Someone searching for "how to budget on low income" is looking for a tool today, not next year.

**Example product type:** Budgeting app or personal finance software.
**Commission potential:** Finance can be one of the high paying affiliate niches, but commissions vary widely between financial products and programs.

**Important:** Financial content should be educational and accurate. Avoid promising guaranteed investment returns or making personal financial claims you cannot support.

### Travel

Travel is another major category in Shopify's 2026 analysis. People search for destinations, hotels, flights, travel equipment, insurance, and activities before taking a trip. Shopify notes that travel and tourism are expected to be a major global market in 2026.

You don't have to become a travel influencer. A small blog can focus on one type of traveler, such as budget travelers, solo travelers, families, or digital nomads. A post like "Best carry-on luggage for budget airlines" can rank and sell without you ever traveling.

**Example product type:** Travel booking service, luggage, or travel insurance.
**Commission potential:** Travel commissions can come from bookings, products, or referrals, with rates depending heavily on the affiliate program.

### Technology, SaaS, and AI

Technology, SaaS, and AI are especially interesting for beginners looking at the best affiliate niches 2026 has to offer. Shopify points to web hosting, marketing software, AI tools, gadgets, and other technology products as potential affiliate opportunities.

The biggest advantage is the constant flow of new products and problems to solve. People want to know which AI tool is useful, which hosting service is suitable for beginners, or which software can make their work easier.

This is also the easiest to start with zero budget because you can review free trials.

**Example product type:** AI writing tool, email marketing platform, web hosting, or productivity software.
**Commission potential:** SaaS programs can offer attractive commissions, and some offer recurring commissions while a referred customer remains subscribed.

### Pets

Pet owners spend money throughout the year on food, toys, training, grooming, healthcare, and accessories. That makes pets a useful evergreen category rather than one that depends entirely on short-term trends.

Shopify's analysis highlights pet food, tracking devices, pet insurance, training, and other pet products as potential affiliate opportunities. A beginner could narrow this down even further. Instead of writing about every animal, you could focus on dogs, cats, aquarium pets, or even one specific breed.

**Example product type:** GPS pet tracker or recurring pet food subscription.
**Commission potential:** Consumable products can create repeat-purchase opportunities, while some services may offer larger referral commissions.

### Health

Health and wellness is a huge category, but it needs to be approached carefully. People search for information about exercise, nutrition, wellness products, healthy routines, and lifestyle improvements every day.

Shopify lists health and wellness among its major affiliate categories, including wellness classes and related products. For a beginner, the safest approach is educational content rather than making medical claims. You can discuss general wellness habits, product features, or questions consumers should ask before buying something.

**Example product type:** Fitness tracker or wellness course.
**Commission potential:** Health-related programs can offer different commission structures, from product percentages to fixed referral payments.

Avoid claiming that a supplement, treatment, or product can cure a medical condition.

### Fitness

Fitness works well for affiliate marketing because there are so many products people compare before buying. Gym equipment, running shoes, fitness trackers, workout programs, and home exercise products all provide potential content ideas.

Shopify specifically identifies workout equipment, running shoes, and wearable fitness devices as examples within the fitness category. You don't necessarily need to be a professional athlete. You could build content around beginner home workouts, walking, or affordable equipment.

**Example product type:** Smart fitness watch or home workout equipment.
**Commission potential:** Commission rates vary, but higher-priced equipment can produce larger commissions per sale even when the percentage is modest.

### Beauty

Beauty continues to be a strong affiliate category because consumers regularly discover products through reviews, tutorials, comparisons, and social content.

Shopify identifies cosmetics, hair products, and beauty technology as opportunities for affiliate marketers. There are also many ways to specialize. You could focus on natural beauty, hair care, skincare routines, makeup for beginners, or affordable beauty products.

**Example product type:** Hair-care product, skincare device, or makeup bundle.
**Commission potential:** Beauty programs can offer competitive percentage commissions, particularly on products with repeat purchases.

### Business and eCommerce

This is one of my favorite categories for beginners because businesses are constantly looking for tools that can save time, increase sales, or make their work easier.

Shopify includes ecommerce platforms, SEO tools, business software, courses, productivity tools, and business books within this category. It also gives you plenty of content ideas. You can write software comparisons, beginner tutorials, tool reviews, and guides explaining how different platforms work.

**Example product type:** Email marketing software, SEO platform, ecommerce tool, or website builder.
**Commission potential:** Business software can be among the high paying affiliate niches, especially when programs offer recurring commissions on subscriptions.

### Fashion

Fashion has always been a large consumer market, but affiliate marketers don't need to compete with huge fashion websites. A more focused approach can work better.

Shopify highlights areas such as sneakers, jewelry, sustainable fashion, capsule wardrobes, and other specialized fashion topics. For example, you could build a website around affordable workwear, minimalist fashion, or sneakers.

**Example product type:** Sneakers, jewelry, handbag, or clothing item.
**Commission potential:** Fashion commissions are usually percentage-based, so earnings depend on the product price and the specific affiliate program.

### DIY

DIY and crafts are great for content creators because the product can naturally become part of the tutorial.

Someone searching for "how to build a small bookshelf" may also need tools, materials, safety equipment, or instructions. Shopify highlights craft supplies, sewing machines, woodworking equipment, jewelry-making supplies, and home improvement products.

**Example product type:** Sewing machine, craft kit, power tool, or woodworking equipment.
**Commission potential:** Higher-priced tools and equipment can provide larger commissions per sale, while smaller craft supplies may benefit from multiple products in one purchase.

### Which Niche Should You Pick If You Have Zero Budget?

If I were starting affiliate marketing today with almost no money, I would look closely at Technology/SaaS/AI and Business/eCommerce.

The reason is simple: you can create useful content around digital products without buying physical inventory. You don't need a warehouse. You don't need to package products. You don't need to ship anything. And you can often create content using free tools while you learn.

Some SaaS affiliate programs also offer recurring commissions. That means the affiliate relationship may continue generating commissions according to the program's terms while the customer remains subscribed. Shopify specifically notes recurring subscription opportunities within technology and business-related affiliate programs.

### Bottom line;

If you've been searching for the best affiliate niches 2026 has to offer, Shopify's latest analysis gives you a useful place to start.

You don't need to build a website about all ten. Pick one. Then choose a smaller sub-niche and start creating helpful content around it. If you're starting with zero budget, Technology/AI and Business/eCommerce are particularly worth exploring because digital products can be promoted without holding inventory.

My advice is this: pick one niche and write five useful articles before worrying about expanding.
