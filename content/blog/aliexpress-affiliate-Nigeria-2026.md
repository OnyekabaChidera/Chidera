## What is AliExpress Affiliate Portal?

It is the official free platform at portal.aliexpress.com. You pick eligible products, create a unique tracking link, and share it.

When someone clicks your link and makes a qualifying purchase within the cookie window (about 3 days), AliExpress tracks it and may pay you a commission.

You earn from sales only. You don't recruit people and you don't handle delivery.

Important: Not every product pays. If the seller is not in the affiliate program, that item pays 0%. Always check the commission rate inside your portal before you share.

## How Much Does AliExpress Affiliate Pay?

There is no fixed 9% for everything. Rates depend on category and campaign.

| Type | What to expect | Normal products | 3% to 9% depending on category |
| Super / Hot Products | Extra bonus on selected items, changes weekly |
| Sale events (11.11, Black Friday) | Rates may go up temporarily |
| Non-participating items | 0%  seller not in program |

Example: A $20 product at 7% commission = $1.40. At 5% = $1.00. To reach the $15 minimum, you need about $200-$300 in qualifying sales at average rates. These are examples, not promises. Your results depend on product, rate, and completed orders.

You may see "50% commission" claims online. That is rare and tied to short campaigns. Don't plan around it.

## How Do Nigerians Get Paid? Opay or Bank?

This is the part most Nigerians search for.

According to AliExpress's published affiliate agreement, commissions are paid monthly for the previous month's qualifying activity, and withdrawals to a designated bank account require a balance exceeding $15. Bank handling fees apply.

What Nigerian affiliates report in practice:

Flow most use: **AliExpress -> Payoneer -> Nigerian Bank / Opay / Palmpay**

Payoneer is free to open. You add your Payoneer details in portal > Payment Settings. When funds enter Payoneer, you withdraw to your local account.

Can you get paid directly to Opay? A direct Opay payout is not guaranteed by AliExpress. The terms say "designated bank account" but don't promise every Nigerian wallet is supported. If Payoneer supports your Opay account number as a withdrawal method, it may work. If not, use a regular bank account first, then move funds to Opay.

Three things to budget for:

- **Delay:** Commissions are confirmed only after buyer receives order and return period ends. Many report 30-60 days pending.
- **Fees:** Payoneer has fees and exchange rate markup. You receive less than dashboard shows.
- **Cancelled orders:** Cancelled or refunded orders may not qualify.

Never pay anyone who promises to "unlock" your earnings.

## AliExpress vs Temu vs ClickBank — Which is Best With $0?

**AliExpress:** Ships to Nigeria, payment via Payoneer to bank/Opay is documented, lower commission but higher trust because Nigerians already buy from AliExpress.

**Temu:** Sometimes higher rates, but delivery to Nigeria is inconsistent and affiliate terms change quickly.

**ClickBank / Digistore24:** Higher commissions (up to 70%) but many offers restrict Nigeria traffic and payouts to Nigeria may be harder.

For beginners in Nigeria starting with $0 and a phone, AliExpress is often the easiest to learn the basics of tracking links and commissions.
