You don't need a paid website, paid ads, or premium tools to start affiliate marketing.

You can start with $0. I know because most beginners waste money on tools before they even get their first click.

If you have time, consistency, and the right free tools, you can start today.

This is the exact stack I would use if I had to start over with nothing.

## What Is Affiliate Marketing In Plain English?

You recommend a product. Someone buys through your special link. The company pays you a small commission.

You don't create the product. You don't handle shipping. Your job is to help someone make a decision.

## Step 1: Pick One Narrow Niche

Don't pick a niche because it pays well. Pick a topic you can talk about for months.

Good niches solve a real problem.

Instead of "fitness" try "home workouts for busy students"
Instead of "tech" try "affordable laptops for remote workers"

Ask yourself: What problem have I solved myself? That's your best niche.

## Step 2: Join Free Affiliate Programs

Joining an affiliate program should always be free. If they ask you to pay to join, walk away.

Look for programs like:
- Amazon Associates for physical products
- Software tools that have affiliate programs - search "[tool name] affiliate program"
- Networks like Impact or PartnerStack to browse offers

Start with 1 or 2 programs. Don't join 20 at once.

Before you promote, check the rules: commission, cookie duration, allowed traffic sources, and payment method.

## Step 3: My Exact $0 Tools Stack

You don't need every tool. You just need tools that do the job.

### 1. Google Docs - For Writing
Write all your articles, scripts, and ideas here. Create separate docs for blog ideas, affiliate links, and drafts. It's free and organized.

### 2. Google Sheets - For Tracking
This is your free dashboard. Create columns for:
Content Title | Affiliate Program | Link | Where Published | Clicks

This keeps you from getting lost.

### 3. Canva Free - For Design
Use the free plan to make Pinterest pins, Instagram posts, blog images, and YouTube thumbnails. Keep designs simple and clean.

### 4. Google Search + AnswerThePublic - For Research
Type your niche into Google. Look at "People also ask" and related searches. Those are real content ideas. AnswerThePublic shows you the exact questions people ask.

### 5. Free Publishing Platform - For Content
You have two options with $0:
Option A: Free blog - Blogger, Medium, or Substack
Option B: One social platform - YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, or Instagram. Pick ONE, not four.

Check the platform's rules about affiliate links. Some allow direct links, others prefer you link to a blog post first.

### 6. Linktree (Free) - For Links
Put all your important links on one page. Easy to share on social profiles.

### 7. Google Search Console + Analytics - For Growth
If you have a blog, these two free tools show you what people search to find you, which pages get clicks, and what content works. No guessing.

## Step 4: Create Helpful Content First

Don't post "Buy this now, it's amazing!" No one clicks that.

Create content that helps first:

- How-to tutorials: "How I set up a free website in 20 minutes"
- Comparisons: "Tool A vs Tool B for beginners"
- Honest reviews: Say what you like and what you don't like

Simple rule: If you wouldn't recommend it to a friend, don't recommend it online.

## Step 5: Promote Without Paying For Ads

Don't buy ads at the start. Use free traffic.

Create useful content on your chosen platform and point people to your helpful article or resource when it makes sense. Never spam your links.

## Step 6: Track and Improve

Check every week:
Which post got the most views?
Which link got the most clicks?
Which content got zero attention?

Make more of what works and fix what doesn't.

## A Simple 30-Day Plan With $0

Week 1: Pick your niche and join 1 affiliate program
Week 2: Set up your free platform and Linktree. Research 5 content ideas
Week 3: Publish 3-5 helpful posts
Week 4: Check your numbers and create more of your best-performing topic

## Mistakes to Avoid

1. Quitting after 2 weeks. Most people stop right before it starts working.
2. Promoting 10 products at once. Focus on 1-2 you understand.
3. Not listening to comments. Comments tell you what to write next.

## Final Thoughts

You can absolutely start affiliate marketing with $0. The tools are free, the platforms are free, the programs are free.

What it costs is time and consistency. You don't need to build everything at once. Start with one niche, one program, one platform, and this free stack.

Publish your first piece this week. It won't be perfect, and that's okay.
