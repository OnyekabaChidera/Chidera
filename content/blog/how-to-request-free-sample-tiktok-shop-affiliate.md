# How to Request Your First Free Sample on TikTok Shop Affiliate: Step-by-Step ($0 Budget)
![Hands unboxing free sample from TikTok Shop Affiliate](/blog/tiktok-shop-free-sample.jpg.jpg)

You want to start TikTok Shop Affiliate but you have no money to buy products. I get it. That's how most of us start.

The good news is? You don't have to buy products to promote them. TikTok has a feature called **Free Sample Request** where brands send you their product for free, and you make a video about it.

This guide will show you exactly how to request your first free sample, even if you are a complete beginner with $0.

> **Disclaimer:** This is an educational content. Commissions vary by product and seller. This post does not guarantee income.

## What is the TikTok Shop Affiliate Marketplace?

Think of it as a big store of products looking for promoters.

Sellers list their products in the **Product Marketplace**. You as a creator pick a product, make a video, add the product link. If someone buys through your video, you earn a commission.

You don't need to:
- Own the product
- Ship anything
- Talk to customers

That's why it's perfect for **zero-budget affiliate marketing**. And the free sample feature is what makes it possible to start with $0.

A free sample is simply a product a seller sends you at no cost so you can test it and create honest content.

## Who Can Actually Request Free Samples? (2026 Update)

Let's be honest here - this part is important.

**1. Country Availability**
TikTok Shop Affiliate is not available everywhere yet. It is fully available in the US, UK, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, and several European countries.

If you are in Nigeria or other African countries, you may not see the Affiliate Center yet. Check your TikTok app first: Go to Profile > Menu > Creator Tools > Look for "TikTok Shop".

**2. Do You Need 1,000 Followers?**
In the US, yes - you usually need 1,000 followers to unlock the affiliate program. In Southeast Asia, rules are different. It depends on your country.

So instead of "no followers needed", the honest truth is "low followers can start, but check your own app".

**3. Do You Need Your Own Shop?**
No. You join as a Creator, not as a Seller. You promote other people's products.

## Step-by-Step: How to Request Your First Sample

### Step 1: Open the Product Marketplace
Open TikTok > Profile > Menu > Creator Tools / TikTok Shop > Affiliate Center > Product Marketplace.

### Step 2: Filter for Free Samples
Inside the marketplace, select filter **"Free Sample"**. 

Pick a niche you can talk about:
- Beauty: makeup brushes, skincare tools
- Tech: phone holders, cables, small gadgets
- Home: kitchen tools, organizers

Start with cheap, small products. They get approved faster.

### Step 3: Check Before You Request
Don't request just because it's free. Check:
- **Commission:** Is it 10% - 20%?
- **Seller Rating:** Is it above 4 stars?
- **Sample Type:** Make sure it says "Free Sample" not "Refundable Sample"

### Step 4: Write a Message That Gets Approved
Click "Request Free Sample". Use one of these templates:

**Template 1 - For US/UK Brands:**
> Hello, I'm a creator in the [your niche] niche and I'm interested in your [product name]. I plan to create a short video showing how it works and my honest experience. I'd love to test it if you approve. Thank you!

**Template 2 - For Beginners:**
> Hi! I love your [product name] - it's perfect for [who it helps]. I'm growing my TikTok account around [your niche] and I make simple, honest review videos. Can I try this and show my audience how it works? Thanks for considering!

**Pro Tip:** Always change the product name and add ONE real detail. Sellers reject copy-paste messages.

### Step 5: Track Your Request
Go to Affiliate Center > Sample Requests. You will see:
- Pending
- Approved (check your shipping address)
- Rejected (normal, try another)

Don't spam 10 requests at once. Start with 1-2.

### Step 6: After Approval
When it arrives, film within a few days. Most sellers expect a video in 7-14 days.

## How to Make Your First Video (No Face Needed)

You don't need to show your face:
- Film your hands unboxing it
- Put it on a table and show how it works
- Add text on screen + voiceover

Hooks that work:
- "I tested this $12 gadget so you don't have to"
- "3 things I liked about this product"
- "Is it actually worth it?"

Then add the product link: Tap "Add Link" > "Product" > Select your product.

## 5 Mistakes to Avoid

1. Requesting 10 expensive products at once
2. Using the exact same message for every seller
3. Ignoring commission rate
4. Not posting the video after getting the sample
5. Making fake claims like "this cures acne"

## FAQ

**Q: Do I have to pay for shipping?**
For a true Free Sample, no. But always read the sample terms.

**Q: Do I need 1,000 followers everywhere?**
No, it varies by country. US generally needs 1,000. Check your app.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
2-7 days on average. Some sellers never reply - that's normal.
